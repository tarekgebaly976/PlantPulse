# PlantPulse cartoon – lock-out tag-out and work permits

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-isolate | anim | Before anyone touches a machine, it is made safe. This work order needs lock-out tag-out.<br>The technician records every isolation point in the LOTO register, as the lock is applied: the point, the type of energy, and the lock and tag number.<br>Then the energy is tested, and Zero energy verified is ticked. |
| 02-signoff | anim | The supervisor signs off the lock-out and the permit on site: the permit number, its types, like hot work, electrical, confined space or work at height, and how long it is valid.<br>Until every point is verified and signed off, the work cannot start: In progress is refused. An expired permit blocks the sign-off and the restart. Only an emergency breakdown may go straight to work. |
| 03-remove | anim | When the work is finished, each lock is removed, and the register keeps who applied it and who removed it. The work order cannot be completed while a lock is still on.<br>And if anything else is missing, like test results or failure codes, the message lists exactly what. Then it can be completed, and the machine runs again. |
| 04-end | anim | Locked, verified, signed off, and released. Safety is built into every work order. |
