# PlantPulse – Skill matrix

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-title | title | The skill matrix. Who can do which job, at what level, and whose certificate is still valid. |
| 02-what | cards | What the skill matrix holds.<br>The plant's own skills: equipment, techniques and safety authorisations.<br>Three levels: basic under supervision, independent, and expert who trains others.<br>Some skills need a certificate with an expiry date.<br>The colour code shows which certificates are valid, expiring or expired.<br>Each skill shows how many people are qualified.<br>Job plans and work orders say which skills they need.<br>Assigning someone without them gives a warning.<br>And the weekly schedule only places qualified people. |
| 03-matrix | App (desktop) – 1 · The matrix | The skill matrix is a tab under Shifts. The engineer keeps it: one row per person, one column per skill.<br>Each cell is the person's level, one to three, coloured by the certificate: green valid, amber expiring within thirty days, red expired or without a date.<br>The last row counts the qualified people per skill. Red means nobody, amber means only one person, so you see where the plant depends on a single technician. |
| 04-cell | App (desktop) – 2 · Level and certificate | Click a cell. Here a confined space certificate expires within thirty days.<br>The level and the date the certificate is valid until.<br>Enter the date of the renewed certificate.<br>Saved. The cell turns green. |
| 05-new | App (desktop) – 3 · New skill | A new skill.<br>The code field is optional now: left blank, it is made automatically from the name.<br>Type the name, choose the category, and say whether a certificate is needed.<br>The skill is a new column in the matrix. |
| 06-wo | App (desktop) – 4 · Required skills | Job plans have a Skills button.<br>It lists the skills the work needs.<br>Add one, with its minimum level.<br>Saved. Work orders made from this plan take these skills with them.<br>This work order, a medium-voltage breaker test, needs the switching authorisation at level two.<br>Assigning a technician whose certificate has expired is stopped, with the reason. A supervisor can still override it on purpose. |
| 07-end | title | That is the skill matrix. Next: the weekly maintenance schedule. |
