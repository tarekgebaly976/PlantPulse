| 02-how | cards | The store in PlantPulse.<br>Each part has a code, description, unit, criticality, and min, max and reorder point.<br>Stock is kept per warehouse: on hand, reserved, available and on order.<br>Goods receipts are booked at moving-average cost, optionally against a requisition.<br>Issues and returns go against a work order, and the cost goes with them.<br>Adjustments always need a reason, and stock can never go negative.<br>Requisitions are local references; there is no ERP connection.<br>Valuation shows the stock value, its aging and excess.<br>And parts used shows what was fitted on every machine. |
