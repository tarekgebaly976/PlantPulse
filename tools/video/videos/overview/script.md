# PlantPulse – overview

| Scene | Shown | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 01-title | title | This is PlantPulse: maintenance and production for an industrial plant, in one place. |
| 02-what | App (desktop) | PlantPulse is a web app for the whole plant: on a computer, a tablet or a phone, in English, Arabic or Bengali. Everyone signs in and sees their own plant, starting with the dashboard. |
| 03-who | cards | Technicians and supervisors run the daily work. Planners and engineers plan maintenance. The store keeps the spare parts, production records its shifts, and managers follow the results. |
| 04-modules | modules | The menu follows the same order. Daily work: requests and work orders. Production: reports, logs and KPIs. Maintenance: preventive plans, lubrication, condition monitoring, shutdowns and root cause analysis. Then assets, spare parts and shifts. And finally, KPIs and administration. |
| 05-colours | App (desktop) | One colour code runs through every screen. Red: act now. Amber: needs attention, or waiting for someone. Green: all clear, or target met. Grey: information. |
| 06-report | App (phone) – 1 · Report | Now one quick story. On line one the press has stopped: its hydraulic unit is leaking oil. The shift leader reports it from a phone, and ticks Breakdown. |
| 07-accept | App (desktop) – 2 · Work order | The mechanical supervisor sees it straight away, accepts the request as an emergency, and a work order is created. |
| 08-safety | App (desktop) – 3 · Safely | Before anyone touches the machine: lock-out tag-out and a work permit. Each isolation point is recorded with its lock, zero energy is verified, and the supervisor signs off. Until then, the work cannot start. |
| 09-work | App (phone) – 4 · The work | The technician starts the job on the phone, writes what was found and what was done, removes the lock, and sends the work for review. |
| 10-close | App (desktop) – 5 · Closed | The supervisor checks the work, adds the failure codes, completes it, and closes it. Every step is kept in the history. |
| 11-kpis | App (desktop) – 6 · KPIs | And it counts. Breakdown downtime, repair time and emergency work are calculated from these records, and every KPI is shown against its target, in the same colours. |
| 12-end | title | PlantPulse: one system, from the shop floor to the KPIs. Next: each module in detail. |
