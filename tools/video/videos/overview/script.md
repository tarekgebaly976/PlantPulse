# PlantPulse – overview

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-title | title | This is PlantPulse: maintenance and production for an industrial plant, in one place. |
| 02-what | App (desktop) | PlantPulse is a web app for the whole plant: on a computer, a tablet or a phone, in English, Arabic or Bengali.<br>Everyone signs in and starts on the dashboard. Each counter opens the list it counts. |
| 03-who | cards | One system, for every role.<br>Technicians do their work on the phone: checklists, hours and spare parts.<br>Supervisors accept requests, assign the work, sign off lock-out and permits, and close the jobs.<br>Planners plan preventive work, schedules and shutdowns.<br>Engineers follow condition monitoring and root cause analysis.<br>The store looks after the spare parts.<br>Production records its shift reports, daily logs and KPIs.<br>And managers follow the KPIs and reports, and give the approvals. |
| 04-modules | modules | The modules follow the menu.<br>Daily work: my work, scan QR, the offline field round, work requests and work orders.<br>Production: the dashboard, reports, daily logs, KPIs, insights and safety incidents.<br>Maintenance: preventive plans, lubrication, condition monitoring, shutdowns and root cause analysis.<br>Then assets, spare parts, and shifts with their handover.<br>And finally: KPIs, reports, and administration. |
| 05-colours | App (desktop) | One colour code runs through every screen, and the dashboard shows it as a key.<br>Red: act now. Amber: needs attention, or waiting for someone. Green: all clear, or target met. Grey: information. |
| 06-report | App (phone) – 1 · Report | Now one quick story. On line one, the press has stopped. The shift leader taps the plus button to report it.<br>First, the equipment: the press hydraulic unit.<br>Then what is wrong: a hydraulic oil leak.<br>The machine is stopped, so Breakdown is ticked.<br>Submit, and the supervisors are alerted at once. |
| 07-accept | App (desktop) – 2 · Work order | The mechanical supervisor opens the new request, marked as a breakdown.<br>They accept it as an emergency breakdown.<br>And the work order is created. |
| 08-safety | App (desktop) – 3 · Safely | Before anyone touches the machine: lock-out tag-out. Each isolation point is written in the register.<br>The lock is applied, and zero energy is verified.<br>Then the supervisor signs off the lock-out and the work permit. Until then, the work cannot start. |
| 09-work | App (phone) – 4 · The work | On the phone, the technician finds the job in My work.<br>They start the work.<br>They write what they found.<br>When the job is finished, the lock is removed.<br>And the work is sent for review. |
| 10-close | App (desktop) – 5 · Closed | The supervisor reviews the work.<br>With the failure codes in, the work order is completed.<br>And closed.<br>Every step is kept in the history, with who did it and when. |
| 11-kpis | App (desktop) – 6 · KPIs | And it counts. Breakdown downtime, repair time and emergency work are calculated from these records.<br>Every KPI is shown against its target, in the same colours: met, close or missed. |
| 12-end | title | PlantPulse: one system, from the shop floor to the KPIs. Next: each module in detail. |
