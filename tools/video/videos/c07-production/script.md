# PlantPulse cartoon – the production day, OEE, paper waste and the cutting plan

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-day | anim | Production records its day in the same system as maintenance. Each line gets a daily report: shift production and machine runs.<br>Every stop and sheet break, with its time, section and responsible discipline.<br>A breakdown stop can raise a maintenance work request with one click.<br>Then the reels, with gross, net, reject and grade, and the quality lines. Totals and OEE are calculated. |
| 02-lock | anim | At the end of the day, the supervisor submits the report. The manager can still correct it, then confirms and locks it, or returns it to draft.<br>Reopening a confirmed day makes a new revision, and the reason is kept. |
| 03-kpis | anim | Production KPIs are worked out from the reports, each against its target: production, OEE and quality.<br>Insights compare the last week with the weeks before, and turn a drift, like a rising reject rate, into an action with an owner.<br>One safety register for the whole plant: days without a lost-time incident, and the rates per million hours worked. |
| 04-cut | anim | Paper waste is read from the reel reports: trim, reject and total waste, for each paper machine, week by week.<br>The cutting plan sets up the winder: the jumbo width, up to six reels a set, and the trim limits. The orders are reels by width, or sheets by width and length.<br>Press Calculate. The optimiser picks the cutting patterns and how many sets of each, and shows the planned trim beside the actual trim from the reports.<br>The converting schedule flags any order that would be late. When the plan is right, it is released, and locked. |
| 05-end | anim | Production and maintenance on the same records, so both sides see the same truth. Next: the dashboard, KPIs and administration. |
