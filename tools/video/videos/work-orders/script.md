# PlantPulse – Work requests and work orders

| Scene | Shown | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 01-title | title | Work requests and work orders. This video follows one job from the moment a problem is reported until the work order is closed, with its hours, parts and costs. |
| 02-flow | cards | Every job follows the same path. A request is reported and accepted. The new work order starts in triage, is approved and assigned, planned and scheduled, then put in progress. When the technician is done it waits for review, and the supervisor completes and closes it. The app checks each step, and only shows the buttons your role is allowed to use. |
| 03-requests | App (desktop) – 1 · Request | Work requests are how anyone reports a problem. The supervisor sees the new requests first, with breakdowns marked. Open one, choose the type and priority, and accept it: the work order is created. A request can also be rejected, with a reason. |
| 04-list | App (desktop) – 2 · The list | The work order list is the planner's desk. Search by number, title or equipment, filter by status and type, or show only open or overdue work. From here you create work orders, import a work plan from Excel, export the list, and assign or close several work orders at once. |
| 05-triage | App (desktop) – 3 · Triage | In triage the planner completes the work order: planned start and end, estimated hours, and the safety needs, lock-out tag-out, a permit, or a test after the work. Then it goes for approval. |
| 06-approve | App (desktop) – 4 · Approve & assign | The supervisor approves it and assigns the technicians. If a technician is on leave, or already booked on another job at the same time, the app says so before you assign. |
| 07-execute | App (desktop) – 5 · The work | The technician starts the work. On the tabs they book their hours, record the spare parts fitted, and write the findings and the downtime. Photos go under attachments. Labor and parts are costed on the work order straight away. |
| 08-review | App (desktop) – 6 · Review & close | Now the supervisor reviews. Completion is refused while something is missing, and the message says exactly what: here, the problem, cause and remedy codes. With the codes in, the work order is completed and closed. The history keeps every step, and the PDF report gives the full work order on one document. |
| 09-end | title | That is the full life of a work order. Next: how technicians do the same work on the phone, even without internet. |
