# PlantPulse – Work requests and work orders

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-title | title | Work requests and work orders. This video follows one job from the moment a problem is reported until the work order is closed, with its hours, parts and costs. |
| 02-flow | cards | Every job follows the same path, and the app only shows the buttons your role may use.<br>A request is reported, and the supervisor accepts or rejects it.<br>The new work order starts in triage, where it is checked and planned.<br>The supervisor approves it and assigns the technicians.<br>The planner sets dates, hours and parts.<br>Work starts, but only after lock-out and the permit are signed off, when they are required.<br>When the technician is done, it waits for review.<br>Completed means codes, root cause and tests are in place.<br>Closed is final, and the job counts in the KPIs. |
| 03-requests | App (desktop) – 1 · Request | Work requests are how anyone reports a problem. The supervisor sees the new ones first, with breakdowns marked.<br>Open a request to see the equipment and what was reported.<br>Choose the type and the priority, and accept it. A request can also be rejected, with a reason.<br>Accepting creates the work order. |
| 04-list | App (desktop) – 2 · The list | The work order list is the planner's desk.<br>Search by number, title or equipment.<br>Filter by status and type, or show only open or overdue work.<br>Tick several work orders to assign, close or cancel them together.<br>From here you also import a work plan from Excel, and export the list. |
| 05-triage | App (desktop) – 3 · Triage | A new work order starts in triage.<br>On the details tab, the planner sets dates, estimated hours, priority and supervisor.<br>And the safety needs: lock-out tag-out, a permit, or a test after the work.<br>Then it is sent for approval. |
| 06-approve | App (desktop) – 4 · Approve & assign | The supervisor approves it.<br>Then chooses the technician.<br>If the technician is on leave, or already booked at the same time, the app says so before assigning. |
| 07-execute | App (desktop) – 5 · The work | With the lock-out signed off, the technician starts the work.<br>On the labor tab, they book their own hours.<br>The hours are costed at once.<br>On the materials tab, they record the spare parts fitted.<br>Search the part by code or description.<br>The part and its cost are on the work order.<br>Then the findings, the root cause and the downtime.<br>The lock is removed, and the work is sent for review. |
| 08-review | App (desktop) – 6 · Review & close | The supervisor tries to complete it. Refused: the message says exactly what is missing, here the failure codes.<br>So the problem, cause and remedy codes are added.<br>Now it can be completed.<br>And closed.<br>The history keeps every step, with who and when.<br>Details shows the hours, the parts and the total cost.<br>And the PDF report gives the whole work order on one document. |
| 09-end | title | That is the full life of a work order. Next: how technicians do the same work on the phone, even without internet. |
