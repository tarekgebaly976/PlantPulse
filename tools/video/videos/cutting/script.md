# PlantPulse – Cutting plan

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-title | title | The cutting plan. Less trim on the winder, and a converting plan that fits. |
| 02-how | cards | How the cutting plan works.<br>The paper waste in the daily reports is the baseline.<br>The winder has its jumbo width and its limits.<br>After it come the sheeters and rewinders.<br>The orders are reels by width, or sheets by width and length.<br>Calculate picks the cutting patterns and how many sets of each.<br>For each sheet order it also picks the unit and the parent reel.<br>And it times the converting against the due dates.<br>The released plan is locked for production. |
| 03-waste | App (desktop) – 1 · Paper waste | It starts with the waste the plant already records. Paper waste, under Production, reads the daily production reports.<br>For each paper machine: trim, reject and total waste, week by week, from the jumbo reels.<br>And by grade, by shift and by cause. This is the baseline before the cutting is optimised. |
| 04-setup | App (desktop) – 2 · Winder and units | The cutting plan is in the Production menu.<br>The winder: the jumbo width, up to six reels a set, and the minimum and maximum trim.<br>After the winder, any number of sheeters and rewinders, each with the sizes it can make and its speed.<br>A cutting plan holds the orders of one machine and one grade.<br>Reel orders by width, sheet orders by width and length, each with a tolerance and a due date. |
| 05-calc | App (desktop) – 3 · Calculate | Press Calculate. The optimiser tries hundreds of cutting patterns and picks the best mix.<br>The planned trim sits beside the actual trim of this grade from the reports, so you see what the plan saves.<br>The cutting patterns: the knife setting of each, how many sets, and the trim.<br>And every reel width: how many are cut, and whether it goes to the customer or to a sheeter or rewinder. |
| 06-converting | App (desktop) – 4 · Converting | For sheet orders, the plan also picks the unit and the parent reel width, so the winder and the converting fit together.<br>The converting schedule shows each unit's jobs in time, from the start of converting, and flags any order that would be late.<br>And the load of each unit, with its size changes. |
| 07-release | App (desktop) – 5 · Release | When the plan is right, release it. A released plan is locked.<br>Orders can also come from Excel: one file can hold several production cycles, and nothing is saved while the file has errors. |
| 08-end | title | That is the cutting plan. Plan the cut, then watch the waste in the reports fall. |
