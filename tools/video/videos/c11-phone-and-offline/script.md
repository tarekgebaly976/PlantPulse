# PlantPulse cartoon – the technician's phone and the offline field round

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-phone | anim | For the technician, PlantPulse lives on the phone. It installs on the home screen and opens on Home.<br>The bar at the bottom holds Home, My work, the plus button, Scan QR and More.<br>My work lists the jobs, soonest first. A mark shows the jobs that need lock-out or a permit. |
| 02-scan | anim | Every machine has a QR label. Scan QR uses the camera, or type the code printed under the label, and the equipment page opens.<br>To report a problem, the phone asks: what do you see or hear? A suspected failure mode is filled in from the answer, and the technician confirms it later on the work order.<br>If the machine is stopped, tick Breakdown, and the supervisors are alerted at once. |
| 03-offline | anim | Some areas have no signal. So the field round is opened once while connected, and the equipment of your departments and your work orders are kept on the phone.<br>Now the signal is gone. The badge turns red, and the round still works. Scan, write a request, and for a stopped machine the phone reminds you to call or use the radio as well.<br>Your own work orders work too: start the work, book hours, add parts, write the findings, and send it for review. It all waits on the phone. Approvals, lock-out sign-off and closing still need a connection. |
| 04-online | anim | As soon as the phone is back online, everything waiting is sent by itself. The request gets its number, each item is sent only once, and the time it was written is kept.<br>If someone changed the same line meanwhile, nothing is overwritten. The phone shows both, with Keep mine or Keep theirs. |
| 05-end | anim | The whole job in the pocket, with or without signal. Next: safety first, lock-out and permits. |
