# PlantPulse – Lubrication

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |

| 01-title | title | Lubrication. Every lubrication point, which lubricant it takes, how much and how often, grouped into routes that become work orders by themselves. |
| 02-how | cards | Lubrication in PlantPulse works like this.<br>Each lubricant has a code, a product, grease or oil, and its viscosity or specification.<br>Each point says which equipment and component, which lubricant, how much, by what method, and how often.<br>Points are grouped into routes, walked together.<br>When points are due, the route becomes one work order.<br>Each point is recorded with the actual quantity.<br>Only the specified lubricant, or an approved alternative, is accepted.<br>Consumption shows how much of each lubricant was used.<br>And QR labels are printed per route. |
| 03-lists | App (desktop) – 1 · Points & routes | The lubrication page has a tab for each list. The points, with their next due date: overdue ones in red.<br>The routes, with their number of points.<br>The tasks, done, missed or pending, each with its work order.<br>The lubricants.<br>And the consumption of each lubricant. |
| 04-route | App (desktop) – 2 · Route work order | Adding a point takes one form.<br>Equipment, component, lubricant, amount, method, how often, and its route. The code is optional: left blank, it is made from the name.<br>Routes are generated automatically, or by the team with Generate.<br>Every due point now has its task and work order.<br>One work order holds every due point of the route.<br>The technician starts the route.<br>A reminder: never mix or substitute lubricants without verified compatibility and approval.<br>Each point is recorded with the quantity actually applied. |
| 05-alternative | App (desktop) – 3 · Alternatives | When another lubricant may be used, an engineer approves it as an alternative.<br>The compatibility evidence must be written.<br>Approved: from now on both are accepted on that point, and nothing else is. |
| 06-labels | App (desktop) – 4 · Labels & import | QR labels can be printed for a whole route, so every point can be scanned in the plant.<br>And existing lubrication plans can be imported from Excel: one row per point, grouped into routes. |
