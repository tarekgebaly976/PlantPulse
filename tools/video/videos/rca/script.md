# PlantPulse – Root cause analysis

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-title | title | Root cause analysis. When a breakdown is serious, or keeps coming back, PlantPulse proposes an analysis, guides the team through it, and checks that the fix really worked. |
| 02-how | cards | From a breakdown to a proven fix.<br>The plant's rules decide what a major breakdown is: downtime for the criticality, repeats, production loss, cost, or a safety event.<br>The manager decides: start the RCA with a leader and team, or say why not.<br>The team investigates: five W's and one H, evidence, fishbone and five whys.<br>They find the root causes: physical, human and system.<br>They plan containment, corrective and preventive actions, with owners and dates.<br>The manager approves, or returns it with a note.<br>Then the effectiveness check: if the same failure comes back, the RCA reopens.<br>And everything prints on one page, with an Excel register. |
| 03-register | App (desktop) – 1 · Register & rules | The register lists every analysis: waiting for a decision, in progress, or closed.<br>Each with its failure mode, why it was started, the leader and the due date. The register exports to Excel.<br>The RCA rules, set by the manager, say what counts as a major breakdown. |
| 04-decision | App (desktop) – 2 · Decision | A rule matched on the grinding mill: high vibration again. The page shows why, and the total impact.<br>The manager starts the RCA with a leader, the team and a due date, or marks it not required, with a reason. |
| 05-investigation | App (desktop) – 3 · Investigation | The investigation follows numbered tabs. One: the problem, in five W's and one H, filled in from the work orders.<br>Three: the fishbone, with possible causes under man, machine, method, material, measurement and environment.<br>Four: the five whys. Keep asking why; the root cause is the last why of each chain.<br>Five: the root causes: physical, human and system.<br>Six: the action plan, with owners and due dates. |
| 06-actions | App (desktop) – 4 · Actions & check | Once approved, the owners carry out their actions, with the result as evidence.<br>An action can raise its own work order; completing the work order completes the action. When the last action is done, the effectiveness check starts, and if the failure returns, the RCA reopens by itself.<br>Print report gives the whole analysis on one page. |
| 07-end | title | That is root cause analysis. Next: the asset register, the equipment tree and QR labels. |
