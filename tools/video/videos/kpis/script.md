# PlantPulse – Dashboard, KPIs and reports

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-title | title | The dashboard, KPIs and reports. Every number is calculated from the records in PlantPulse, and every number can be opened down to those records. |
| 02-dashboard | App (desktop) – 1 · Dashboard | The Home page is the first screen, the plant's dashboard: quick buttons, counters, the work that needs attention, KPI tiles and condition alarms.<br>Every counter uses the colour code and opens exactly the list it counts.<br>Each person can customise it: sections, counters and KPIs. The layout is saved on the server, so phone and computer show the same. |
| 03-search | App (desktop) – 2 · Search & notifications | The magnifier, or Control K, searches everything you may see at once.<br>Type a code, a name, or a few words in any order: equipment, work orders, requests, parts, plans, RCAs and shutdowns.<br>Notifications collect alerts, approvals and assignments for you. |
| 04-kpis | App (desktop) – 3 · KPIs | KPIs and reports shows twenty maintenance KPIs for any period and department, calculated from the records.<br>Each one against its target: green met, amber close, red missed. Missing data says Not available, and why; nothing is estimated.<br>Open a KPI: its formula, the records behind it, and a twelve-month control chart with the target.<br>The plant's own KPI sheet goes in Company KPIs, entered or uploaded from Excel, month by month. |
| 05-reports | App (desktop) – 4 · Reports | Production and budget data are entered here, for cost per ton, OEE and budget against actual.<br>Every KPI exports to Excel and PDF.<br>And the management presentation builds a PowerPoint deck in your language, with the scorecard, trends, bad actors, costs and the plan for the next period. |
| 06-end | title | That is the dashboard, KPIs and reports. Next: administration. |
