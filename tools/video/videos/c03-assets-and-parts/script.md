# PlantPulse cartoon – equipment, QR labels and spare parts

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-tree | anim | Every machine in the plant lives in one tree: plant, area, line, equipment and component.<br>Each one has a criticality, A, B or C. It drives the priorities and the rules for root cause analysis.<br>And each one gets a QR label. Printed on sheets of twenty-four, stuck on the machine, and scanned with any phone. |
| 02-page | anim | Scan it, and the machine's own page opens: its details, maker, model and running hours, and what it cost to maintain.<br>Its full history, every work order, with failures and reliability over twelve months.<br>The spare parts that fit it, its meters and condition points, its lubrication, and its manuals and drawings.<br>And a problem can be reported from the page itself, in seconds. |
| 03-store | anim | Spare parts live in the store. Each part has a minimum, a maximum and a reorder point, and critical spares are marked.<br>Stock is kept per warehouse: on hand, reserved, available and on order.<br>The storekeeper books goods receipts, and the average cost is updated by itself. A correction always needs a reason, and stock can never go negative. |
| 04-issue | anim | Parts are issued against a work order, and their cost goes with them onto the job.<br>Requisitions are kept as local references with the purchase order number. There is no ERP connection.<br>Valuation shows the value of the stock, aging and excess show what has not moved, and parts used shows what was fitted on every machine. |
| 05-end | anim | Every machine with its own page, and every part where it should be. Next: the people, their shifts and their skills. |
