# PlantPulse cartoon – preventive maintenance and lubrication

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-plans | anim | Preventive maintenance works in two steps. A job plan describes the work: the tasks, readings with limits, safety precautions and tools.<br>A PM plan links it to a machine and says how often: by the calendar or by running hours, from a fixed date or from the last completion.<br>PlantPulse then creates the work order a set number of days before it is due, never twice, with the tasks as its checklist. |
| 02-do | anim | On the day, the technician opens it on the phone and works through the checklist. A reading out of range is flagged at once.<br>Done on time, within its tolerance, it counts in PM compliance.<br>If a PM cannot be done on its date, the planner asks for a deferral with a reason, and the manager approves it. Nobody approves their own request. |
| 03-lube | anim | Lubrication works the same way. Each point says which lubricant, how much, by what method and how often.<br>Points are grouped into routes. When points are due, the route becomes one work order.<br>The technician records each point with the quantity actually applied. Only the specified lubricant, or an approved alternative, is accepted.<br>Consumption shows how much of each lubricant was used, and QR labels are printed for each route. |
| 04-end | anim | Plan it once, and PlantPulse brings the work on time, with the right lubricant every time. Next: equipment, QR labels and spare parts. |
