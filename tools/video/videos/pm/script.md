# PlantPulse – Preventive maintenance

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-title | title | Preventive maintenance. You describe the job once, say how often it is due, and PlantPulse creates the work orders on time, with the checklist inside. |
| 02-how | cards | Preventive maintenance works in two steps.<br>A job plan describes the work: the tasks, readings with limits, safety precautions and tools.<br>A PM plan links the job plan to a piece of equipment, and says how often.<br>By the calendar, or by running hours.<br>From a fixed date, or from the last completion.<br>The work order is created a set number of days before it is due, and never twice.<br>The job plan's tasks become its checklist.<br>Done on time, within the tolerance, it counts in PM compliance.<br>And deferring or cancelling a PM needs a second person to approve. |
| 03-plans | App (desktop) – 1 · Plans | This is the planner's page. PM plans lists every plan, with its next due date and last completion.<br>Job plans hold the task lists.<br>A new job plan takes its tasks one by one: a check, or a reading with a unit and limits, plus safety precautions and tools.<br>A new PM plan picks the equipment, the job plan, the trigger, the frequency, and how many days ahead the work order is made. |
| 04-generate | App (desktop) – 2 · Work orders | The scheduler makes the work orders every day. A planner can also run it now.<br>The schedule tab shows what is due, with a link to each work order.<br>Open one.<br>The job plan's tasks are its checklist: OK or not OK, and readings that are flagged when out of range. |
| 05-waiting | App (desktop) – 3 · Auto or manual | Each department chooses: Auto, the system creates the work orders, or Manual. The same switch exists for condition monitoring rounds and lubrication routes.<br>In Manual, the occurrences wait in Waiting for release, and the team releases them when it suits the plan. |
| 06-deferral | App (desktop) – 4 · Deferral | Sometimes a PM cannot be done on its date. The planner requests a deferral.<br>With a reason and a new date.<br>The manager sees it waiting in Approvals.<br>And approves it. Nobody can approve their own request. |
| 07-import | App (desktop) – 5 · Excel | Existing plans can be imported from Excel: download the template, one row per task, preview, then import. Nothing is saved if a row has an error.<br>Export plans gives every plan, plus the next sixty days of work, ready to plan and import back as a work plan. |
| 08-end | title | That is preventive maintenance. Next: lubrication, with its points, routes and the right lubricant every time. |
