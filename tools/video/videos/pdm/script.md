# PlantPulse – Condition monitoring

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-title | title | Condition monitoring. Vibration, temperature, current and other readings are compared with their limits, so a problem is seen and planned before it becomes a breakdown. |
| 02-how | cards | Condition monitoring works like this.<br>Each measurement point has a technique, a unit, and warning and alarm limits.<br>Readings are entered on site, from a round, or received from the plant.<br>Each reading is compared with the limits: normal, warning or alarm. A plain limit check, no prediction is claimed.<br>The control chart shows the last hundred readings, with the eight Nelson rules.<br>Passing a limit raises an alert.<br>The alert can become a predictive work order.<br>And it is resolved with what was done.<br>Reading rounds become work orders, just like preventive maintenance. |
| 03-points | App (desktop) – 1 · Points | The points list shows the last value and the level of each point, worst first. The Nelson column shows patterns, like a steady rise, even inside the limits.<br>Click a point.<br>Its control chart: the average, the control limits, the warning and alarm limits, and every Nelson rule that is broken. |
| 04-reading | App (desktop) – 2 · A reading | Here is the blower bearing.<br>Recording a reading takes one number.<br>The answer comes at once: the warning limit is passed. An alert is raised, and the dashboard counter goes up. |
| 05-alerts | App (desktop) – 3 · Alerts | The alerts tab lists the open alerts.<br>Filtered by level: here, only the alarm limit.<br>Open the alert.<br>The engineer acknowledges it.<br>And creates a predictive work order with one click.<br>Here it is. When the work is done, the alert is resolved with what was found. |
| 06-excel | App (desktop) – 4 · Excel | Points and their rounds can be imported from Excel, and so can the history of past readings, so the trends start full.<br>And a priority list in Excel sets which points matter most. |
| 07-end | title | That is condition monitoring. Next: shutdowns, planned as work packages on a Gantt chart. |
