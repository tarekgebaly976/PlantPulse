# PlantPulse – PDCA, continuous improvement

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-title | title | To finish: how PlantPulse supports continuous improvement, with the Plan, Do, Check, Act cycle. |
| 02-cycle | cards | PDCA is a simple loop: plan, do, check, act, and again. PlantPulse gives each step its tools.<br>Plan: preventive and lubrication plans, the weekly schedule, shutdowns, the cutting plan, and a target for every KPI.<br>Do: work orders with lock-out and permits, daily production reports, and field rounds on the phone.<br>Check: KPIs against targets, control charts with Nelson rules, insights, alerts and paper waste.<br>Act: RCA actions, changed plans, effectiveness checks and approvals. |
| 03-plan | App (desktop) – Plan | Plan. Preventive maintenance, lubrication routes and shutdowns are planned in advance, and the work orders come by themselves on time.<br>The weekly schedule puts the approved work on people who are at work and qualified, approved by the engineer and the maintenance manager.<br>Production plans the winder's cutting for the least trim.<br>And every KPI has a target, for the plant or each department. The plan says what good looks like. |
| 04-do | App (desktop) – Do | Do. The work is done through work orders: approved, assigned, made safe with lock-out and permits, and recorded with hours, parts and findings, on the computer or the phone.<br>Production records every day the same way: output, stops, losses and quality. |
| 05-check | App (desktop) – Check | Check. Every KPI is calculated from those records and shown against its target, in the colour code.<br>The control chart and the Nelson rules show whether a change is real, or just noise.<br>Insights and alerts find drifts and repeated losses before they become big problems.<br>And paper waste shows, week by week, whether the cutting plan really cuts the trim. |
| 06-act | App (desktop) – Act | Act. A major or repeated failure gets a root cause analysis, with corrective and preventive actions, owners and due dates.<br>Changes to plans, like a deferral or a cancellation, are approved by a second person, and kept in the history. |
| 07-loop | cards | And the loop closes.<br>What is learned changes the plan: a better PM plan, or a new target, starts the next cycle.<br>The effectiveness check proves it: if the same failure returns, the analysis reopens by itself.<br>Maintenance and production work on the same records, so everyone checks the same truth.<br>Small cycles every day on the Home page, and bigger ones in the monthly review with the management presentation. |
| 08-end | title | PlantPulse: plan the work, do it safely, check the results, and act on them. One system, every day. |
