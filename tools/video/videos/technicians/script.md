# PlantPulse – Technicians on the phone

| Scene | Shown | Narration (one line per step) |
|---|---|---|
| 01-title | title | This video is for technicians. Everything they need is on the phone: their jobs, the equipment behind every QR label, and a field round that keeps working without internet. |
| 02-mywork | App (phone) – 1 · My work | PlantPulse installs on the phone's home screen and opens on Home, with the work that needs attention. The bar at the bottom holds Home, My work, the plus button, Scan QR and More. My work lists your jobs, soonest first; a red shield marks jobs that need lock-out or a permit.<br>Open a job.<br>The buttons at the bottom move it on: start the work.<br>The job is in progress. Its tabs hold the checklist, hours and parts. |
| 03-scan | App (phone) – 2 · Scan QR | Every machine has a QR label. Scan QR uses the phone's camera.<br>Or type the code printed under the label.<br>The equipment page opens.<br>Its history, spare parts and documents, and a button to report a problem. |
| 04-prepare | App (phone) – 3 · Prepare | Some areas have no signal, and the field round is made for that. Open it once while connected: all equipment of your departments and your work orders are kept on the phone.<br>Update refreshes them at any time. |
| 05-offline-request | App (phone) – 4 · No signal | Now the signal is gone. The badge turns red, and the round still works.<br>Search, or scan, the equipment.<br>Its card shows the last work orders, as downloaded.<br>Write a new work request.<br>Tick Breakdown. For a stopped machine the app reminds you to call or use the radio as well.<br>It is saved on the phone, waiting to be sent. |
| 06-offline-wo | App (phone) – 5 · Work offline | Your own work orders work offline too.<br>Open one.<br>Start the work, without internet.<br>Book your hours.<br>Add the parts you fitted.<br>Write your findings.<br>And send it for review. It waits on the phone. Approvals, lock-out sign-off and closing still need a connection. |
| 07-online | App (phone) – 6 · Back online | Two items are waiting on this phone.<br>As soon as the phone is back online, they are sent by themselves. The request gets its number, each item is sent only once, and the time it was written is kept. |
| 08-end | title | That is the technician's day on the phone. Next: preventive maintenance, and how planned work is created by itself. |
